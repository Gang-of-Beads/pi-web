Saving the gateway server settings in Settings → General no longer erases your Lists choice or a hand-set `logging`, and saving any settings card no longer erases a hand-set `environmentFacts`. The Lists card marks nothing until you choose, so either option can be picked from the start; after a failed save it goes back to the saved choice and shows its own error. Restart the web process, then reload the browser.
