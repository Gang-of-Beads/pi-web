Keep the navigation key in the window's top-left corner and Go to in its top-right on every layout, whether or not a session is open. On desktop the navigation key opens and closes the navigation panel, and Go to opens the page column with its list or closes the column; click the page's name at the top of the column to switch pages without closing it. A page shown full-window keeps both keys, so there is always a way back. The arrows on the desktop side panels' edges are gone: drag a panel's edge to resize it, or double-click the edge to reset its width. On a phone with no conversation open, the navigation key stays in its corner.
