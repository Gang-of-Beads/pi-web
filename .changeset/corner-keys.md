Keep the navigation key in the window's top-left corner and Go to in its top-right on every layout, whether or not a session is open. Go to opens the Go to list and closes whatever it shows, the list or a page, and the next Go to starts from the list again; on a wide desktop window the list and its pages sit in the column beside the conversation, and on narrower windows the list is a menu and its pages take the conversation's place. A page opened from Go to has a Back key in its top-left corner that returns to the list; on a phone it takes the navigation key's place, and the Go to menu carries the navigation key. A page shown full-window keeps these keys, so there is always a way back. Settings' back key on a phone looks the same. The arrows on the desktop side panels' edges are gone: drag a panel's edge to resize it, or double-click the edge to reset its width. On a phone with no conversation open, the navigation key stays in its corner. The keys in every top bar now line up with the messages' edges.
