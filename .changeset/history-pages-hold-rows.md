Scrolling back through a session with long agent turns holds your place better when a page of history lands. An earlier page is now at most about two pages instead of the whole rest of a long turn, so it loads faster and no longer pushes the rows you are reading out of the window in the long sessions we tested, and rows that join an open group of tool steps above you no longer push you down. On a phone, the transcript no longer jumps under your finger when rows leave the top of the window.
