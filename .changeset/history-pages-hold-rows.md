Scrolling through a long session keeps the rows you are reading when a page of history lands. A page inside a long agent turn is no longer the whole turn, so it no longer pushes your rows out of the window and throws you elsewhere, and such pages load faster. On a phone, the transcript no longer jumps under your finger when rows leave the top of the window.
