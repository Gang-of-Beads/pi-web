A conversation row keeps its place on screen when a row above it changes. A message leaving the waiting block, a retried attempt being hidden, or a withdrawn message used to renumber every row below it: the page rebuilt those rows, and a saved reading position could name a different row. Rows are now named by what they are. After the upgrade, a session's saved reading position opens at the bottom once, and an event group you had opened shows closed once. Restart the web service.
