A dialog an extension opened leaves nothing in the chat once it closes, as in pi's terminal: the "Cancelled", "Timed out" and "Answered" cards that sat at the bottom of the chat under every later message are gone. When a tool asked, the tool's own row says what happened: the result an extension draws for its tool (such as "Cancelled") now shows under the call, as pi's terminal shows it, instead of inside the folded Details, which keeps the tool's raw output. Reload the page to apply.
