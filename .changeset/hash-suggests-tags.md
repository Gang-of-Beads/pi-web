Typing `#` in the Navigate search now suggests the tags your sessions carry, with how many sessions carry each; tap one to search by it. Sessions are also tagged `working`, `idle` and `archived`, so `#idle` or `#archived` finds them (`#running` is now `#working`, the word the row shows). Restart the web service to pick it up.
