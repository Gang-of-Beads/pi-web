Plugins can now bring global pages: pages about the machine on screen that need no project, beside the project pages they had. Go to offers the pages of what you are looking at: on the Navigate page its switch decides (a project, or All projects), and anywhere else what is open decides (a project's session or page offers its project pages; nothing open, or a global page, offers the global pages). On a desktop the right-hand column opens for a global page even with no workspace selected. The machine also gets terminals of its own, in its home folder, for global pages. Restart the web process, then reload the browser.
