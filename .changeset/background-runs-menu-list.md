Background runs have their own list in the ≡ menu, drawn by the background-runs plugin: running work first, then the newest finished runs with name (up to two lines), status, duration and exit code laid out apart, and a long history cut to the newest thirty with the rest counted. The panel is there whenever a session is open, like Subagents and Tasks, and says honestly whether the runs are still being read, could not be read, or are none; nothing is drawn above the composer.
