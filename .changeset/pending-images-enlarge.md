Enlarge a pending image attachment. The thumbnails next to the composer were inert pictures: tapping one did nothing, and a keyboard user had no way to see the image at full size. Each thumbnail is now a real button that opens the image in its own dialog, with the close action reachable by keyboard and by Esc, and the backdrop clickable to dismiss.
