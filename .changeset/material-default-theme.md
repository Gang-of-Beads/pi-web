Use Material Dark and Material Light as the built-in appearance. A saved Pro or Pro Light choice moves to the matching Material side, and Pro now ships in the Themes plugin. Plugin themes can declare `replacedBy` so an older entry gives way to a newer one without appearing twice in Settings. Update the Themes plugin to 0.1.1 or later so Material is listed once.
