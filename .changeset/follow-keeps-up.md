A conversation you follow now stays at the newest messages when several arrive at once, such as follow-ups answered together, instead of stopping about a screen short with the rest piling up below. Only your own scrolling stops following (a wheel, a drag, the keys, or keyboard focus moved up into the conversation); a jump that comes with no input of yours, such as find in page, is brought back to the newest messages.
