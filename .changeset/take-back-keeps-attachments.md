Keep a message's attachments when it comes back to the composer. Taking a message back, Stop, Clear queue and aborting a tree navigation now return its images and unsaved files, not just its words, and the Stop shortcut no longer drops queued messages. Prompt history remembers the attachments of recent inputs, including image-only ones, for the arrow keys and the history sheet. When this browser has no copy of a message's images (one queued from another device), the composer says how many did not come back instead of returning the words as if that were all.
