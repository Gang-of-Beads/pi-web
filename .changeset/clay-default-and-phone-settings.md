Clay becomes the default theme: a fresh browser follows the system light/dark preference through the clay pair (Clay in the dark, Clay Paper in the daylight) instead of the PI WEB pair, and a stored preference still wins. The settings dialog drops its desktop accent on phones: section tabs become single-line pills that meet the 44px touch floor, field labels lose their all-caps styling, and the close control meets the touch floor too.
