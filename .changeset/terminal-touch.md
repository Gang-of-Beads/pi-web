The terminal page works the way phone terminals do. A shell tab is its name and a close button; right-click a tab on a desktop to rename or close it, and long-press it on a phone to rename it. On a phone, long-press the terminal's text to select a word with the system's handles and Copy menu, and tap to return to the live terminal; a key pressed while text is selected still goes to the shell, and the selection highlight no longer hides the text under it. The extra keys always show on touch screens, and a floating button raises the phone's keyboard and hides while it is open. The Select and Keys buttons are gone. When the machine does not answer, the page keeps the shells it shows, says "Reconnecting…", and tries again by itself instead of showing a red error, and a dropped shell connection reconnects.
