The Pinned sessions on the Navigate page can be put in your own order by dragging. On a desktop, press a pinned session and drag it; a click still opens it. On a phone, hold a session until batch mode opens and keep dragging, or in batch mode rest a finger on a pinned session for a moment and then drag. The order is kept on the machine, so every device shows it, and a pinned session no longer moves when it works or has a new reply. Restart the web service to pick it up.
