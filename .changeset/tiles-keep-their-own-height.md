Tile grids keep their rows as tall as the tiles. The first botim-eclipse visit showed why this matters: its 291 workspaces collapsed into stacked two-pixel tracks, each tile painted over by the next, and the page read as a deck of empty card tops with no label anywhere. Any list longer than one grid row was affected; shorter lists hid the bug.
