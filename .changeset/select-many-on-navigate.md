On the Navigate page you can select several sessions or projects and act on them together. On a phone, hold a row for half a second; on a desktop, Shift- or Cmd/Ctrl-click a row or press Select. The page then shows only the selection: a way out (✕, Escape or back), the count, Select all, and the actions that fit (Archive, Pin, Unpin, Mark as read; Restore and Delete permanently for archived sessions; Pin, Unpin and Close project for projects). Delete permanently and Close project ask first. Restart the web service to pick it up.
