A session whose folder is gone is now listed in Archived, pinned or not, with "Folder missing" under its name: in Navigate instead of among your active or pinned sessions, where it could not open, and in session search, which did not list it at all. Nothing is moved: its pins and files stay, and it returns to its place once the folder is back. Tapping it opens its menu, which offers **Continue in another folder…**: the whole conversation is copied as a new session into a folder of the project you choose that exists - a project whose own folder is gone is offered too, for a live folder of its own, and the copy opens once it is written, leaving the original where it is. In Navigate the menu also keeps its pins and offers Mark as read when it is unread, which is new, and Archive when it can be archived; once archived it opens read-only, as any archived session does. Session search's menu offers Continue and its global pin. To a plugin's sections and labels such a session reads as archived, as it is listed. A session saved by an older pi is upgraded in the copy. A folder PI WEB cannot look at, such as one on a network mount that stopped answering, no longer counts as gone: its sessions stay where they are, and starting, continuing or opening a terminal there says the folder could not be looked at.
