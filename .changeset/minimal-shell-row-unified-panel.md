The shell's resident row shrinks to three controls: the panel toggle, the session name, and the working indicator. Quick access, machine/project/workspace switching, workspace tool views, actions, settings, and the app refresh move into the single collapsible panel (side panel on desktop, full-page panel on mobile), and the mobile tool sheet is retired. Every row control meets the 44px touch floor, the bar no longer scrolls sideways, and the bottom status bar is unchanged.
