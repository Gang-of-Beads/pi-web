The pre-ask void judges messages by identity, not words. A captionless photo or a template the runtime expanded still voids the form it predates, and a remark whose words collide with something queued earlier no longer closes the form - the id is the key, text only a fallback for senders without one. The reload banner also stops treating the server's placeholder version as an update.
