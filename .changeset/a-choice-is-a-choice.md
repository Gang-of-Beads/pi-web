Choosing a theme means getting that theme. Picking Clay Paper under system dark used to render the dark pair member with a "chosen, but following your system" label - a choice something else could override. An explicit pick now turns system-following off; the Auto toggle re-arms it deliberately. A re-pick of the active theme also stops repainting the whole document for nothing.
