Sessions can be archived and deleted again from the Sessions page. A session's ⋯ menu offers Archive. Archived sessions move to a collapsed "Archived (N)" group at the bottom of the list, where each one offers Restore and Delete permanently, which asks first. A second tap on a row's ⋯ now closes its menu on phones as well, instead of closing and reopening it.
